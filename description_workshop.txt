[h2] 普通的NPC - Normal NPC [/h2]

如其名，只是个普通的NPC模组，类似其他NPC模组：
[list]
    [*][b] 添加六名NPC以售卖原版和模组中各种各样的物品。 [/b]
    [*][b] 添加了原版和模组内大量物品的合成配方。 [/b]
    [*][b] 添加了一些提高游戏体验的新物品。 [/b]
    [*] 以及其他功能……
[/list]

As the name suggests, it is just an normal NPC mod, similar to other NPC mods:
[*][b] Adds six NPCs that sell a wide variety of Vanilla and Mod items.  [/b]
[*][b] Adds crafting recipes for a large number of Vanilla and Mod items.  [/b]
[*][b] Adds several new items that improve the game experience.  [/b]
[*] And other functions...
